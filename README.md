# StudyMate AI 🎓

### Your Personalized Learning Hub & Smart Study Planner

StudyMate AI is an interactive learning and productivity platform designed to help students and aspiring professionals learn new technologies, organize their tasks, track progress, and achieve their career goals.

## 🚀 Features

- **Career-Based Learning:** Explore courses based on your chosen technology and career path.
- **Interactive Learning Hub:** Access courses in Data Science, Full Stack Development, Python, UI/UX Design, Digital Marketing, AI/ML, Cloud Computing, and more.
- **Smart Priority Tasks:** Organize tasks by priority, deadlines, and completion status.
- **Upcoming Exams & Deadlines:** Keep track of assessments, project submissions, and important dates.
- **Personalized Recommendations:** Discover your next learning activity based on your selected career and progress.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor completed modules and learning milestones.
- **Study Streak & Focus Timer:** Build consistent study habits and manage your study sessions.
- **Responsive Dashboard:** Access the learning platform on desktop and mobile devices.
